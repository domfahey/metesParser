**Transfer of Ownership.** The Grantor grants and conveys (transfers ownership of) the property described below to the Grantee. This transfer is made for the sum of **FIVE HUNDRED AND FORTY-EIGHT THOUSAND FIVE HUNDRED DOLLARS ($548,500.00).** The Grantor acknowledges receipt of this money.

**Tax Map Reference.** (N.J.S.A. 46:15-1.1) Borough of Haddonfield, Block 64.23, Lot 22.

> _SEE EXHIBIT "A" ATTACHED HERETO AND MADE A PART HEREOF_

**BEING** the same land and premises which became vested in Noreen Lanza Living Trust Dated October 24, 2014, by deed from Noreen C. Lanza dated December 16, 2014 and recorded with Camden County Recording Office on January 5, 2015, in Book 10129, Page 454 ($1.00 deed).

**Promises by Grantor.** The Grantor promises that the Grantor has done no act to encumber the property. This promise is called a "covenant as to grantor's acts" (N.J.S.A. 46:4-6). This promise means that the Grantor has not allowed anyone else to obtain any legal rights which affect the property (such as by making a mortgage or allowing a judgment to be entered against the Grantor).

**Signatures.** The Grantor signs this Deed as of the date at the top of the first page.
