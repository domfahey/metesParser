Promises by Grantor. The Grantors’ promises are listed below. Each promise is expressed in the language of a New Jersey law (with a reference to the law) and is followed by an explanation in plain language. The Grantors promise that:

* a. The Grantors are lawfully seized of the said land (N.J.S.A. 46:4-3) - the Grantors are the legal owners;
* b. The Grantors have the right to convey the said land to the Grantees (N.J.S.A. 46:4-4) - the Grantors have the right to convey (sell) this property;
* c. The Grantees shall have quiet possession of the land (N.J.S.A. 46:4-5) - the Grantees will not be disturbed by others with claims against this property;
* d. The Grantors will execute such further assurances of the said lands as may be requisite (N.J.S.A. 46:4-10) - the Grantors will comply with the Grantees’ reasonable requests to correct any title defect; and
* e. The Grantors will warrant generally the property hereby conveyed (N.J.S.A. 46:4-7) - the Grantors guaranty the Grantees’ ownership of the property, but only to the Grantees and their personal representatives.

Who is Bound. The promises made in this Deed are legally binding upon the Grantors and all who lawfully succeed to the Grantors’ rights and responsibilities. These promises can be enforced by the Grantees and all future owners of the property.
