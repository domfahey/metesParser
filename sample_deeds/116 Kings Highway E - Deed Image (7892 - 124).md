# CORRECTIVE DEED

This Deed is made as of the 27th day of June, 2005,

## BETWEEN

116 KINGS HIGHWAY EAST ASSOCIATES, L.L.C.,

a New Jersey limited liability company, having a mailing address of c/o The Streamwood company, 89 Haddon Avenue, Suite 8, Haddonfield, New Jersey 08033 (herein referred to as the "Grantor"); and

116 KINGS HIGHWAY ASSOCIATES, L.L.C.

a New Jersey limited liability company, having a mailing address of c/o The Streamwood company, 89 Haddon Avenue, Suite 8, Haddonfield, New Jersey 08033 (herein referred to as the "Grantee").

## Transfer of Ownership.

The Grantor hereby quitclaims, grants, conveys, transfers and sets-over unto the Grantee the lands and premises ("Property") described below. This Deed is made and given in consideration of the payment of One Dollar ($1.00), and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt of which is hereby acknowledged by the Grantor.

## Property.

The Property is situate in the Borough of Haddonfield, County of Camden, and State of New Jersey, and is shown on the Tax Map of said Borough as Block 34, Lot 6. The Property is more fully described on Exhibit "A" attached hereto and made a part hereof.

On the 15th day of October 2002, Hudson City Savings Bank did execute and deliver to "116 Kings Highway East Associates, L.L.C." a deed of conveyance for the Property in the said Borough of Haddonfield herein described, which deed was recorded in the Office of the Camden County Clerk on November 13, 2002 in Deed Book 5264, at Page 849. However, through inadvertence and mistake the said deed of conveyance sets forth the name of the Grantee as aforesaid when the name of the Grantee should be "116 KINGS HIGHWAY ASSOCIATES, L.L.C." as set forth herein. The present Deed is being recorded to correct the previous Deed. The consideration recited in the incorrect Deed shall sustain the present Deed.

## Promises by Grantor.
The Grantor promises that the Grantor has done no act to encumber the Property. This promise is called a "covenant as to grantor's acts" (N.J.S.A. 46:4-6). This promise means that the Grantor has not allowed anyone else to obtain any legal rights which affect the Property (such as by making a mortgage or allowing a judgment to be entered against the Grantor).
