# CONFIRMATORY D E E D

This Deed is made on May 9, 1996

BETWEEN ONE CENTENNIAL SQUARE ASSOCIATES, L.L.C., a New Jersey limited liability company, having its principal office at Suite 103, One Centennial Square, Haddonfield, New Jersey 08033, referred to as the Grantor.

AND ONE CENTENNIAL SQUARE ASSOCIATES, L.L.C., a New Jersey limited liability company, having its principal office at Suite 103, One Centennial Square, Haddonfield, New Jersey 08033, referred to as the Grantee.

## Transfer of Ownership
The Grantor grants and conveys (transfers ownership of) the property described below to the Grantee. This transfer is made for the sum of ONE DOLLAR ($1.00). The Grantor acknowledges receipt of this money.

## Tax Map Reference
(N.J.S.A. 46:15-2.1) Municipality of Haddonfield, Block No. 129, Lot No. 3.

## Purpose
The purpose of this Deed is to confirm of record the merger of One Centennial Square Associates, a New Jersey general partnership and prior owner of the property, into One Centennial Square Associates, L.L.C., a New Jersey limited liability company and current owner of the property, which merger occurred on April 29, 1996, as evidenced by Certificate of Merger of that date, filed in the New Jersey Secretary of State's Office on May 8, 1996.

## Property
The property consists of the land and all the buildings and structures on the land in the Borough of Haddonfield, County of Camden, and State of New Jersey. The legal description is attached hereto and made a part hereof as Exhibit "A".

## Promises by Grantor
The Grantor promises that the Grantor has done no act to encumber the property. This promise is called a "covenant as to grantor's acts" (N.J.S.A. 46:4-6). This promise means that the Grantor has not allowed anyone else to obtain any legal rights which affect the property (such as by making a mortgage or allowing a judgment to be entered against the Grantor).
