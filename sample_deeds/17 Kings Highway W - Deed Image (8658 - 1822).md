# CORRECTIVE DEED

This Corrective Deed is made on August 15, 2007, to be effective as of the 31st day of August, 2006, between **WACHOVIA BANK, NATIONAL ASSOCIATION**, a national banking association having an address at 123 South Broad Street, PA 4840, Philadelphia, PA 19109, referred to as the Grantor, ultimate successor by one or more mergers to Covenant Bank For Savings, and **FIRST STATES INVESTORS 3613, LLC**, a New Jersey limited liability company, having its principal office at 610 Old York Road, Suite 300, Jenkintown, PA 19046, referred to as the Grantee.

## Transfer of Ownership

The Grantor grants and conveys (transfers ownership of) all of its right, title and interest in the property (called the "Property") described below to the Grantee. This transfer is made for the sum of ONE DOLLAR ($1.00). The Grantor acknowledges receipt of this money.

## Property

SEE EXHIBIT "A" ANNEXED HERETO AND MADE A PART HEREOF.

BEING known as 17 Kings Highway West.

BEING the same lands and premises granted and conveyed unto First States Investors 3613, LLC, by Deed from Wachovia Bank, National Association dated August 31, 2006, and recorded in the Camden County Clerk’s Office on March 9, 2007, in Deed Book 8494, Page 812.

Exhibit "A" of the aforementioned Deed from Wachovia Bank, National Association contained an incorrect distance reading of "90.72 feet" in the second call, which should instead read "80.72 feet". The purpose of this Corrective Deed is to revise the second call of the legal description as set forth on Exhibit "A" attached hereto and made a part hereof.

## Promises by Grantor

The Grantor promises that the Grantor has done no act to encumber the Property. This promise is called a "covenant as to grantor’s acts" (N.J.S.A. 46:4-6). This promise means that the Grantor has not allowed anyone else to obtain any legal rights which affect the Property (such as by making a mortgage or allowing a judgment to be entered against the Grantor).
