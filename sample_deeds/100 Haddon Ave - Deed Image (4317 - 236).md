## Promises by Grantor
The Grantor promises that the Grantor has done no act to encumber the property. This promise is called a "covenant as to grantor's acts" (N.J.S.A. 46:4-6). This promise means that the Grantor has not allowed anyone else to obtain any legal right which affects the property (such as by making a mortgage or allowing a judgment to be entered against the Grantor).
