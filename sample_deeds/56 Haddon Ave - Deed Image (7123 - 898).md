## Transfer of Ownership
The Grantor grants and conveys (transfers ownership of) the property described below to the Grantee. This transfer is made for the sum of THREE MILLION FIVE HUNDRED THOUSAND DOLLARS ($3,500,000.00). The Grantor acknowledges receipt of this money.

## Tax Map Reference
(N.J.S.A. 46:15-2.1) Municipality of Haddonfield, Block 22, Lot No. 1.01.

## Property
The property consists of the land and all the buildings and structures on the land in the Borough of Haddonfield, County of Camden, and State of New Jersey. The legal description is:

SEE EXHIBIT "A" ATTACHED HERETO AND MADE A PART HEREOF

BEING the same land and premises which became vested in UMRE Corp. by Deed from UM Holdings, Ltd., a New Jersey corporation, dated March 26, 1996, recorded in the Office of the Clerk of Camden County on March 27, 1996, in Deed Book 4810, Page 61 &c.

## Promises by Grantor
The Grantor promises that the Grantor has done no act to encumber the property. This promise is called a "covenant as to grantor's acts" (N.J.S.A. 46:4-6). This promise means that the Grantor has not allowed anyone else to obtain any legal rights which affect the property (such as by making a mortgage or allowing a judgment to be entered against the Grantor).
