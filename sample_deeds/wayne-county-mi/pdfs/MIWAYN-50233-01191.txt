THIS MEMORANDUM OF LEASE (“Memorandum”) is executed as of Chae 12 2012, by ALDI INC.
(MICHIGAN), a Michigan corporation (“Lessee”).

WHEREAS, Lessee and CANTON SHOPPING CENTER COMPANY, L.L.C., a Michigan limited liability
company (“Lessor”), entered into a Lease dated as of December 19, 2007 (the ““Lease”), for the rental of approximately
18,737 rentable square feet of real property located at 42053 Ford Road in the Township of Canton, Wayne County,
Michigan (the “Premises”), together with all buildings, fixtures not leased from other parties, and other improvements
located thereon and all rights appurtenant thereto, which premises is part of the real property more particularly
described on Exhibit A attached hereto and made a part hereof.

All capitalized terms used in this Memorandum which are not defined in this Memorandum shall have the
meanings ascribed to such terms in the Lease

THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants contained in the Lease and other good
and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, Lessor and Lessee state and
agree as follows:

1. BASE TERM. The Term of the Lease has a Commencement Date of September |, 2008 and the
expiration date of the Base Term will be September 30, 2023.

2, OPTIONS TO EXTEND TERM OF LEASE. The Lease grants to Lessee the right to extend the Term
of the lease for up to three (3) consecutive five (5) year periods.

3. LEASE. The Lease fully sets forth the names and addresses of the parties thereto and contains all of
the terms, covenants, conditions and agreements to be performed by the parties thereto. The purpose of this
Memorandum of Lease is to put all parties on notice of Lessce’s rights and interest under said Lease with respect to the
Premises. In the event of a conflict between the terms of the Lease and this Memorandum of Lease, the terms of the
Lease shall control
