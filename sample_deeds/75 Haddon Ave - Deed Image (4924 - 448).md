## Promises by Grantor
The Grantor promises that the Grantor has done no act to encumber the Property. This promise is called a "covenant as to grantor's acts" (N.J.S.A. 46:4-6). This promise means that the Grantor has not allowed anyone else to obtain any legal rights which affect the Property (such as by making a mortgage or allowing a judgment to be entered against the Grantor).

## Promise of Limited Warranty by Grantor
The Grantor promises that the Grantor will warrant and defend the Property unto the Grantee against the claims and demands of all persons whomsoever; provided, however that the Grantor's liability hereunder is hereby limited to the amount that the Grantor can collect under its title insurance policy covering the Property. The promise of warranty contained in the immediately preceding sentence is limited to the Grantee named herein and may be enforced only by such Grantee, and not by its assigns or any future owners of the Property.
