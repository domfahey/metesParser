# DEED

This Deed is made on January 3, 2012,

BETWEEN ONE CENTENNIAL SQUARE ASSOCIATES, L.L.C., a New Jersey limited liability company, whose address is One Centennial Square, Haddonfield, New Jersey 08033, referred to as the Grantor,

AND CENTENNIAL OFFICE LLC, a Pennsylvania limited liability company, whose address is 1636 Lois Lane, Bethlehem, Pennsylvania 18018, referred to as the Grantee.

The words "Grantor" and "Grantee" shall mean all Grantors and all Grantees listed above.

Transfer of Ownership. The Grantor grants and conveys (transfers ownership of) the property described below to the Grantee. This transfer is made for the sum of NINE MILLION THREE HUNDRED FIFTY THOUSAND DOLLARS ($9,350,000.00). The Grantor acknowledges receipt of this money.

Tax Map Reference. (N.J.S.A. 46:15-1.1) Municipality of Haddonfield, Block No. 129, Lot No. 3.

Property. The property consists of the land and all the buildings and structures on the land in the Borough of Haddonfield, County of Camden, and State of New Jersey, more commonly known as One Centennial Square, bounded and described as follows:

SEE EXHIBIT "A" ATTACHED HERETO AND MADE A PART HEREOF.

SUBJECT to all easements and restrictions of record.

BEING the same land and premises conveyed unto Grantor by Deed from One Centennial Square Associates, L.L.C., dated May 9, 1996, recorded May 22, 1996, in the Camden County Clerk's Office in Deed Book 4819, beginning at page 618.

BEING also the same land and premises vested in One Centennial Square Associates, a New Jersey partnership, by Deed from Patrick J. Ruane and Charles G. Harrison, a New Jersey partnership t/a The Centennial Square Building, dated March 1, 1983, recorded March 9, 1983, in the Camden County Clerk's Office in Deed Book 3882, beginning at page 600.

Promises by Grantor. The Grantor promises that the Grantor has done no act to encumber the property. This promise is called a "covenant as to grantor's acts" (N.J.S.A. 46:4-6). This promise means that the Grantor has not allowed anyone else to obtain any legal rights which affect the property (such as by making a mortgage or allowing a judgment to be entered against the Grantor).
