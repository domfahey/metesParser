This Deed is made on October 20, 2017.

## BETWEEN:

CATHERINE V. STRANQUIST AND EVELYN PENZA, CO-EXECUTORS  
OF THE ESTATE OF JO ANN VERDI  
whose address is: 210 E. Center Street, Moorestown, New Jersey 08057, referred to as the Grantor,

AND

GJN REALTY, LLC  
whose address is: 489 Barnsboro Road, Sewell, New Jersey 08080, referred to as the Grantee.

The words "Grantor" and "Grantee" shall mean all Grantors and Grantees listed above.

1. Grantor. The Grantor makes this Deed as the Co-Personal Representatives of the Estate of Jo Ann Verdi who died on August 16, 2016, a resident of the County of Burlington, State of New Jersey. Letters were issued to the Grantor herein by the Surrogate of Burlington County on September 21, 2016.

2. Transfer of Ownership. The Grantor grants and conveys (transfers ownership of) the property (called the "Property") described below to the Grantee. This transfer is made for the sum of Eight Hundred Forty Five Thousand Five Hundred ($845,500.00) Dollars. The Grantor acknowledges receipt of this money.

# 4. Property.
The property consists of all that certain tract or parcel of land and premises situate in the Borough of Haddonfield, County of Camden, State of New Jersey, described as follows:

See Attached Exhibit A

The street address of the property is: 15 East Euclid Avenue, Haddonfield, New Jersey 08033

BEING the same land and premises that became vested in Jo Ann Verdi by Deed dated May 7, 2007 from Catherine Verdi Gorman and Francis P. Verdi, Trustees of the Jo Ann Verdi Revocable Trust dated March 29, 2004, and recorded on June 13, 2007 in Deed Book 8577, Page 1165, in the office of the Camden County Clerk/Registrar’s Office.

# 5. Promises by Grantor.
The Grantor promises that the Grantor has done no act to encumber the Property. This promise is called a "covenant as to grantor’s acts" (N.J.S.A. 46:4-6). This promise means that the Grantor has not allowed anyone else to obtain any legal rights which affect the Property (such as by making a mortgage or allowing a judgment to be entered against the Grantor).

# 6. Signatures.
This Grantor signs this Deed as of the date at the top of the first page. If the Grantor is a corporation, this Deed is signed and attested by its proper corporate officers and its corporate seal is affixed.

# EXHIBIT A

## LEGAL DESCRIPTION

ALL that certain lot, piece or parcel of land, with the buildings and improvements thereon erected, situate, lying and being in the Borough of Haddonfield, in the County of Camden, State of New Jersey:

### Tract #1

Beginning in the Northwesterly line of Euclid Avenue 80 feet Southwestwardly from the Westerly corner of Euclid and Turnley Avenues; thence Southwestwardly along the Northwesterly line of Euclid Avenue 80 feet in width or breadth; thence Northwestwardly between parallel lines, parallel with Turnley Avenue 142 feet in length or depth.

### Tract #2

Beginning at a point in the boundary line of premises owned by the party of the first part which beginning point is located on the Northerly line of Euclid Avenue, distant 160 feet Westwardly along said line from its intersection with the Westerly line of Turnley Avenue; and extends thence

1. Westwardly along said Northerly line of Euclid Avenue the distance of 10 feet to a point; and extends thence  
2. Northwardly parallel with the Westerly line of Turnley Avenue making an interior angle of 89 degrees, 43 minutes 30 seconds with the preceding course the distance of 142 feet to a point; and extends thence  
3. Eastwardly parallel with the first course the distance of 10 feet to an angle in the boundary line of property of the party of the first part; and extends thence  
4. Southwardly parallel with the second course along party of the first part’s said boundary line the distance of 142 feet to the point of beginning.

FOR INFORMATION PURPOSES ONLY: BEING known as 15 East Euclid Avenue, Tax Lot 3.01, Tax Block 129 on the Official Tax Map of Borough of Haddonfield, New Jersey.
