## Transfer of Ownership

The Grantor grants and conveys (transfers ownership of) the property described below to the Grantee. This transfer is made for the sum of SEVEN HUNDRED FIFTY THOUSAND DOLLARS ($750,000.00). The Grantor acknowledges receipt of this money.

## Property

The property consists of the land and all the buildings and structures on the land in the Borough of Haddonfield, County of Camden, and State of New Jersey. The legal description is:

See Schedule A attached hereto and made a part hereof.

BEING the same land and premises conveyed unto Lillian Ragone from Daniel Ragone, by Deed dated December 31, 1994 and recorded in the Office of the Register of Deeds of Camden County on May 23, 1995 in Deed Book 4600, Page 702.

## Promises by Grantor

The Grantor promises that the Grantor has done no act to encumber the property. This promise is called a "covenant as to grantor's acts" (N.J.S.A. 46:4-6). This promise means that the Grantor has not allowed anyone else to obtain any legal rights which affect the property (such as by making a mortgage or allowing a judgment to be entered against the Grantor).
