# DEED
## Bargain and Sale
with Covenants as to Grantor's Acts

This Deed is made on the 24 day of August, 2023, between,

20 EAST REDMAN AVENUE, LLC, a New Jersey Limited Liability Company, by Michael McLaughlin, Managing Member, whose address is 130 N. Haddon Avenue, Haddonfield, New Jersey 08033, referred to as the Grantor, and,

4547 WALNUT LLC, whose address will be 313 Redman Avenue, Haddonfield, New Jersey 08033, referred to as the Grantee.

The words "Grantor" and "Grantee" shall mean all Grantor and Grantees listed above.

Transfer of Ownership. The Grantor grants and conveys (transfer ownership) of the property described below to the Grantee. This transfer is made for the sum of ONE MILLION THREE HUNDRED THOUSAND DOLLARS ($1,300,000.00). The Grantor acknowledges receipt of this money.

Tax Map Reference. (N.J.S.A. 46:15-1.1) Borough of Haddonfield, Block No. 129, Lot No. 2.

Property. The property consists of the land and all the buildings and structures on the land in the Borough of Haddonfield, County of Camden and State of New Jersey. The legal description is attached hereto.

BEING the same lands and premises which became vested in 20 East Redman Avenue, LLC, a New Jersey Limited Liability Company, by Deed from Edgepark Drive Associates, LLC, a New Jersey Limited Liability Company, dated May 10, 2006, recorded May 25, 2006, in the Camden County Clerk/Register’s Office in Deed Book 8214, Page 294, Instrument No. 2006061109.

Promises by Grantor. The Grantor promises that the Grantor has done no act to encumber the property. This is called a "covenant as to grantor's acts" (N.J.S.A. 46:4-6). This promise means that the Grantor has not allowed anyone else to obtain any legal rights which affect the property (such as by making a mortgage or allowing a judgment to be entered against the Grantor).
