# DEED

THIS DEED is made on January 4, 2010,

BETWEEN 116 KINGS HIGHWAY ASSOCIATES, L.L.C., a New Jersey limited liability company, having an address at 89 Haddon Avenue North, Suite B, Haddonfield, New Jersey 08033 (the "Grantor") AND HADDONFIELD MALL EQUITIES, LLC, a New Jersey limited liability company, having an address at 17000 Horizon Way, Suite 100, Mount Laurel, New Jersey 08054 (the "Grantee").

## 1. Transfer of Ownership.
The Grantor grants and conveys (transfers ownership of) the property described below (the "Property") to the Grantee. This transfer is made for the sum of TWO MILLION EIGHT HUNDRED SEVENTY-FIVE THOUSAND AND 00/100 DOLLARS ($2,875,000.00). Grantor acknowledges receipt of this money.

## 2. Tax Map Reference.
(N.J.S.A. 46:15-1.1) Borough of Haddonfield, Block No. 34, Lot No. 6.

## 3. Property.
The Property consists of the land and all the buildings and structures on the land in the Borough of Haddonfield, County of Camden and State of New Jersey. The legal description of the Property is attached hereto as Exhibit "A" and made a part hereof.
