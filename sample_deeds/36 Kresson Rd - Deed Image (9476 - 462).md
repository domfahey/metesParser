# CONFIRMATORY DEED

THIS CONFIRMATORY DEED is made as of June 13, 2011.

## BETWEEN

Gitomer & Martin, a New Jersey General Partnership, also known as Gitomer-Martin Properties, having an address at PO Box 424, Mt. Laurel Township, Burlington County, New Jersey, referred to as the "Grantor";

## AND

Irving Gitomer, having an address at, 301 Browning Lane, Unit 50, Cherry Hill Township, Camden County, New Jersey, referred to as the "Grantee".

Transfer of Ownership. Grantor grants and conveys (transfers ownership of) the property described below to Grantee. This transfer is made for the sum of One and 00/100 Dollars ($1.00). Grantor acknowledges receipt of this money.

Tax Map Reference. The Property being transferred hereby consists of certain real property located in the Township of Cherry Hill, Camden County, State of New Jersey and is designated on the Municipal Tax Map of the Township of Cherry Hill as Block 423.01, Lot 4, commonly known as 36 Kresson Road, Cherry Hill, New Jersey (the "Property").

Property. The legal description of the Property is attached hereto as Exhibit "A".

Being the same land and premises which became vested in Gitomer & Martin, a Partnership, by deed from Anthony E. Calderetti and Theresa Calderetti, his wife, dated August 17, 1981, recorded on August 21, 1981, in the Camden County Clerk's Office in Deed Book 3794, Page 373 et seq.

Confirmatory Deed. The purpose of this Confirmatory Deed is to confirm that title is vested solely in Grantee, Irving Gitomer, the only individual with a partnership interest in Gitomer & Martin, and that all prior partners other than Irving Gitomer have assigned their interests in Gitomer & Martin to Irving Gitomer, the above named Grantee.

Promises by Grantor. Grantor promises that, except as set forth in this Deed, Grantor has done no act to encumber the Property. This promise is called a "covenant as to grantor's acts" (N.J.S.A. 46:4-6). This promise means that Grantor has not allowed anyone else to obtain any legal rights, which affect the Property (such as by making a mortgage or allowing a judgment to be entered against Grantor), except as stated above.
